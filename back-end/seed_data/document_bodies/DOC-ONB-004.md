# Front of House Service Standards

## 1) Purpose and Scope
- This is the onboarding standard for Hearthline Front of House.
- Owner: Grace Liu (FOH Lead).
- Your job: greet fast, confirm allergies every time, run food by seat, and keep the pass tight with proper expo etiquette.
- Non‑negotiables from the start:
  - Greet every table within 60 seconds.
  - Ask every table: "Does anyone at the table have a food allergy?" Enter allergies as the first modifier.
  - Run food in order of seat number; call the dish name as you set it down.
  - Never promise an item is allergen-free — check with the kitchen.

## 2) Pre-Shift Setup and Readiness
- Uniform: clean, pressed, closed-toe, hair tied back, minimal scent.
- Tools:
  - Pens (x2), notepad, wine key, lighter, crumb tool, clean side towel.
  - Sanitizer bucket at station, dry towels for glassware only.
  - Full water carafe and backup at side stand; ice bin topped.
- Dining room:
  - Tables level, chairs wiped, menus clean (no tears, no grease).
  - Seat numbers marked mentally before service: pivot seat is 1; number clockwise.
- POS:
  - Log in, test printer, paper loaded.
  - Review 86’d items, features, allergens flagged by kitchen notes.
- Communication:
  - Check with expo for call‑outs (timing, coursing, fires).
  - Verify runner rotation and who is on window.

## 3) Guest Flow: From Greeting to Payment
1. Approach within 60 seconds. Smile, eye contact, welcome, water or beverage offer.
2. Ask clearly: "Does anyone at the table have a food allergy?" Enter allergies as the first modifier.
3. Mark seat numbers quietly (use pivot) so you can run food in order of seat number; call the dish name as you set it down.
4. Take drink order, repeat back. Ring immediately.
5. Present menu features and timing honestly. Never promise an item is allergen-free — check with the kitchen.
6. Take food order by seat, repeat back, confirm modifiers and doneness.
7. Ring orders in course order; allergies as the first modifier; double‑check seat numbers.
8. Pre‑stage share plates and utensils before food lands if needed.
9. Two‑minute/first‑bite check. Refill waters, maintain table.
10. Offer dessert or coffee once mains are cleared; then handle check promptly.

## 4) Timing and Pacing Targets
- Hit these unless expo calls an adjustment. Communicate slips early.

| Step | Target Time | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Greeting | 60 s | Non-negotiable |
| Non-alc drinks | 3 min | Water down immediately |
| Cocktails/wine | 5 min | Stemware polished |
| Starters on table | 8–10 min | Stagger if sharing |
| Mains on table | 12–18 min | Course fire via expo |
| 2‑min check | 2 min | After first bite |
| Dessert on table | 8–12 min | Coffee with dessert |
| Check drop | 2 min | After dessert offer declined |
| Payment close | 3 min | Return cards at table |

## 5) Allergy Protocol (Zero Slip)
- Ask every table: "Does anyone at the table have a food allergy?" Enter allergies as the first modifier.
- If any allergy:
  1. Clarify ingredient and severity (ingestion vs. airborne; cross‑contact sensitivity).
  2. Check with the kitchen. Never promise an item is allergen-free — check with the kitchen.
  3. Confirm safe options with expo; ring with allergy as the first modifier and seat number.
  4. Mark the ticket “ALLERGY” in POS, red chit if applicable; verbally confirm at the pass.
  5. When running, announce “Allergy order, Seat X” to expo; use clean tray, clean hands.
- If a guest reports an allergy after ordering:
  - Stop the fire. Tell expo and the manager immediately. Re‑ring corrected items per protocol.
- If a reaction occurs:
  - Call manager. Call emergency services if guest requests or shows distress. Stay calm, get ingredient list from expo.

## 6) Expo Etiquette and Food Running
1. At the pass, listen first; expo leads the window.
2. Tickets:
   - Hand tickets to expo flat, top-right corner aligned, speak seat numbers and allergy flags.
   - Do not stack tickets over hot window slots.
3. Hands and calls:
   - Answer “Hands” calls fast. Announce “hot behind,” “sharp,” “corner.”
   - Wipe rims, check garnishes, confirm temps verbally with expo if asked.
4. Running:
   - Run food in order of seat number; call the dish name as you set it down.
   - Never auction food. If lost, step back, confirm quietly, then reset.
5. Do not cross the pass for garnishes; ask expo. Keep the window clear and dry.
6. Return trays and tray jacks clean. Replace dead tickets in bin, not on counter.

## 7) Order Entry and Seat Discipline
- Seat map:
  - Pivot is seat 1 (closest to you at initial greet). Number clockwise.
  - Large parties: confirm pivot with a teammate before entering.
- POS steps:
  1. Select table, assign seats.
  2. Enter allergies as the first modifier.
  3. Enter items by seat; add temps, modifiers, coursing.
  4. Double‑check chit: seats, modifiers, allergy tag.
  5. Send; tell expo about fires or holds.

## 8) Sidework and Cleanliness
- Opening:
  - Polish 24 wine stems, 24 rocks, 24 water glasses.
  - Fold 40 napkins; stock silver, share plates, ramekins.
  - Fill water station, brew coffee/tea mise, restock sugars and creamers.
- During:
  - Keep sections crumbed, waters full, floors dry.
  - Bus to designated tubs: glass, flatware, plate scrap.
- Closing:
  - Reset tables, polish glassware used, wipe menus.
  - Sanitize side stands; empty and flip ice, wipe mats.
  - Count and restock disposables, check linen return.

## 9) Troubleshooting and What To Do If
- Food running delay:
  - Check chit time at expo, update guest with a real window, offer bread or water top‑off.
- Wrong seat drop:
  - Own it. Retrieve quietly, confirm seat, re‑drop with apology.
- 86’d item after order:
  - Offer two alternatives similar in style/price. Confirm timing before promising.
- Guest not ready to order:
  - Offer a small share or drink refresher. Step away, return in 3–4 minutes.
- Drink remake:
  - Remove, apologize, remake ticket “REMAKE” with reason; manager comp if needed.
- Spills/breakage:
  - Announce “glass,” block area, sweep and mop, change guest items affected.

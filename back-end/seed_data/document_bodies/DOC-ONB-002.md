# Grill Station Orientation

## Owner and Purpose
- Document owner: Priya Nair (Sous Chef, Grill)
- Purpose: Station map, heat zones, ticket flow and mise en place for new grill cooks. How to set up, cook consistently, communicate with Expo, and close down safely.

## Station Map and Heat Zones
Left third: sear (650°F+). Middle: cook. Right: hold/rest.

- Visual map (left to right):
  - Left third = Sear zone (high flame, dry grates). For initial marks and crust.
  - Middle = Cook zone (steady heat for bringing items to temp).
  - Right = Hold/rest zone (lowest heat or coolest corner). For carryover and staging just before plate-up.
- Manage zones:
  - Preheat at least 20 minutes before service; confirm left third reads 650°F+.
  - Keep left third clean and lightly oiled; carbonized grates = bitter flavor.
  - Keep right side clear of direct flames; use this zone to rest proteins and avoid overcooking.

## Mise en Place Checklist
Proteins portioned and labelled · chimichurri (fresh) · finishing salt · herb oil · fry baskets · clean towels in sanitizer (red bucket, 200 ppm quat).

Add these supports so service runs clean:
- Tools: 2 long tongs (label for raw/cooked), 1 fish spatula, bench scraper, grill brush, probe thermometer, timer, squeeze bottles (herb oil), hotel pans with wire racks, parchment for resting board.
- Backups: extra clean towels, fresh sanitizer test strips, extra probe batteries, foil.
- Labels: date/time initials on all open items; raw vs cooked containers kept apart and below/above per Food Code.
- Fire control: salt for flare-ups (never water).

## Ticket Flow and Communication
Expo calls, grill calls back the count, fires on “Order, fire!”. Temps are read with a probe — never by touch.

- Call-backs:
  - On “Order!” repeat item and count: “Heard, two strip, one salmon.”
  - Fire only when you hear “Order, fire!” from Expo.
- Mid-cook updates: call “two minutes out strip” or “need 90 seconds salmon” so Expo can pace sides.
- Probe use:
  - Insert from the side into the thermal center; avoid bone and grill marks.
  - Wipe probe with sani towel between checks.

## Cooking Standards and Doneness Targets
- Season at the board, not over the grill. Pat dry for better sear.
- First contact on the left third for crust; move to middle to finish; rest on the right.
- Resting: most cuts rest 2–4 minutes on rack or board before plate.
- Use the table below as house targets; confirm all with probe.

| Item         | Target Internal Temp | Notes                                                                 |
|--------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Steak rare   | 125°F                 | Hard sear, short middle time; rest 2 min.                             |
| Steak med-rare | 130–135°F          | Standard for most steaks unless guest requests otherwise.             |
| Steak medium | 140–145°F            | Keep middle zone steady; rest 3 min.                                  |
| Steak med-well | 150–155°F          | Longer middle time; avoid left zone after first sear.                 |
| Steak well   | 160°F+               | Use right zone to prevent charring; extend rest.                      |
| Burger       | 155°F                 | Press with spatula only for contact; never squeeze juices out.        |
| Chicken      | 165°F                 | Finish in middle; rest 3–5 min to carryover and set juices.           |
| Salmon       | 125–130°F            | Pull slightly under; carryover on right zone achieves final.          |
| Pork chop    | 145°F                | 3 min rest minimum.                                                   |

## Setup Procedure (Pre-Service)
1. Wash hands; set red bucket at 200 ppm quat; verify with test strip.
2. Light grill; preheat all zones, then tune: left third high (650°F+), middle medium, right low.
3. Brush and oil grates: brush, wipe with lightly oiled towel, repeat until clean and glossy.
4. Set boards: raw board left, cooked/plating board right. Separate tongs labeled for raw/cooked.
5. Stage mise: proteins portioned and labelled, chimichurri (fresh), finishing salt, herb oil, fry baskets, clean towels in sanitizer (red bucket, 200 ppm quat).
6. Thermometer check: ice water slurry should read 32°F; if off, calibrate or swap.
7. Fire test: sear a trim piece to confirm crust in 30–45 seconds on left third.

## Service Flow (During)
1. On call, repeat count; fire only on “Order, fire!”.
2. Sear on left third; set cross marks by rotating 45° after initial color if requested.
3. Move to middle to reach target temp; check with probe; call time updates to Expo.
4. Rest on right zone; glaze/finish: herb oil and finishing salt as directed on spec.
5. Plate hot; hand to Expo; reset grates with a quick brush swipe.
6. Keep station wiped; switch towels if soiled; maintain sanitizer at 200 ppm.

## Food Safety and Allergen Control
- Cold hold 41°F or below; hot hold 135°F or above.
- Reheat cooked items to 165°F before hot holding.
- Raw below ready-to-eat; cooked tools never touch raw product.
- Allergens: clean grate area (brush, wipe, oil), fresh towel, fresh tongs, and new pan for any allergen ticket. Verbally confirm “allergen grill clear” to Expo.

## Troubleshooting and What To Do If
- Flare-ups: move item to middle or right; kill flame briefly; salt the fire if needed; brush grate, re-oil.
- Grill too cool: confirm gas/airflow, clear debris, increase preheat. If left third isn’t 650°F+, alert Priya Nair immediately.
- Uneven cooking: rotate item 180°, shift to a hotter/cooler lane; use a rack on right to prevent scorching.
- Behind on fires: tell Expo “grill is three minutes heavy”; prioritize shortest-cook proteins first.
- Running low on proteins: call pars to Prep; 86 only via Chef/Expo confirmation.
- Probe reading jumps: replace battery or grab backup; never guess doneness by touch.
- Chimichurri looks oxidized: pull and replace with fresh; do not serve dull or brown sauce.

## Close-Down and Cleaning
1. Land last tickets; move grates to high to carbonize residue 5–10 minutes.
2. Brush thoroughly; cool slightly; wipe with lightly oiled towel to season grates.
3. Power down gas; close valves. Empty drip tray into approved waste.
4. Discard any time/temperature abused items; label and chill safe leftovers promptly.
5. Buckets: dump, rinse, make fresh sanitizer (200 ppm quat); wipe all contact surfaces.
6. Tools: wash-rinse-sanitize and air-dry; store raw and cooked tool sets separately.
7. Restock for next shift: towels, test strips, fuel checks, backup probes. Report any maintenance needs to Priya Nair.

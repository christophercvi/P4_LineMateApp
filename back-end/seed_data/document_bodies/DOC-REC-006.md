# House Vinaigrettes

Owner: Hannah Becker (Prep Cook). Station: Prep. Scope: Sherry-shallot, Champagne-herb, Lemon-dijon. Ratio: 3:1 oil to acid. FIFO. Clean as you go.

## Core Recipe Specs
Short notes: Keep the exact specs below. Do not round or substitute without chef approval. Follow dressing-specific shelf life.

| Dressing | Acid | Oil | Emulsifier | Shelf life |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sherry-shallot | Sherry vinegar 250 ml | Grapeseed 750 ml | Dijon 30 g | 7 days |
| Champagne-herb | Champagne vinegar 250 ml | Olive 750 ml | Honey 40 g | 5 days (fresh herbs) |
| Lemon-dijon | Lemon juice 250 ml | Olive 750 ml | Dijon 60 g | 3 days |

Label, date and store at 41°F or below.

## Batch Scaling and US Equivalents
Short notes: Use a scale for grams. Volumes are shown in US and metric for speed. Keep 3:1 oil-to-acid. Yields are approximate.

| Dressing | Acid (US approx) | Oil (US approx) | Emulsifier (US approx) | Target Yield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sherry-shallot | 250 ml ≈ 1 cup + 2 tbsp | 750 ml ≈ 3 cups + 2 tbsp | 30 g ≈ 1 oz (≈2 tbsp Dijon) | ~1 qt |

| Lemon-dijon | 250 ml ≈ 1 cup + 2 tbsp | 750 ml ≈ 3 cups + 2 tbsp | 60 g ≈ 2.1 oz (≈4 tbsp Dijon) | ~1 qt |

## Equipment and Setup
Short notes: Sanitize before use. Cold ingredients make a more stable emulsion.

- Gear: 2 mixing bowls, whisk or immersion blender, fine-mesh strainer, microplane, cutting board, chef knife, scale, measuring cups/spoons, funnel, 1 qt cambros with lids, 16 oz squeeze bottles, labels, marker.
- Mise: Oils and acids measured, emulsifiers weighed, herbs picked/chopped for Champagne-herb, shallots minced for Sherry-shallot, lemons juiced/strained for Lemon-dijon.
- Allergen: Dijon contains mustard; honey is not vegan.

## Procedure (All Three)
1) Sanitize station. Wash hands. Set up labeled cambros and squeeze bottles.
2) Combine acid + emulsifier in a bowl: whisk until smooth. For honey, warm slightly if crystallized (do not exceed room temp).
3) Slowly stream in oil while whisking to emulsify. For immersion blender: pulse on low, move gently to avoid aeration.
4) Season to taste with kosher salt and black pepper. Do not add water.
5) For Champagne-herb: fold in chopped fresh herbs at the end; do not blend herbs.
6) Taste check: balance should be bright, not sharp; texture should coat a spoon with no oil pooling.
7) Transfer through funnel to labeled containers. Cap immediately. Refrigerate.

## QC, Labeling, and Holding
Short notes: Follow strict shelf life by dressing. If in doubt, throw it out.

| Checkpoint | Standard | Action if Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Emulsion | Uniform, glossy, no visible oil split after 5 minutes | Re-emulsify: whisk or blend; if still split, remake |
| Aroma | Clean acid, no sulfur/yeast off-notes | Discard if off |
| Color | True to style; herbs bright green | If herbs dull/gray, discard and remake |
| Labeling | Name, maker initials, time, shelf life end | Relabel immediately; if unknown date, discard |
| Temp | 41°F or below at all times | Rapid chill, investigate walk-in; discard if held above 41°F >4 hours |

## Troubleshooting and What-To-Do-If
- Split vinaigrette: Add 1 tsp acid to a clean bowl; slowly whisk in split vinaigrette to bring it back. If it won’t hold, remake.
- Too sharp/acidic: Whisk in oil in small additions to maintain 3:1.
- Too flat/oily: Add acid in small additions; recheck salt.
- Bitterness (olive oil): Blend less, whisk by hand, or switch to a milder olive oil next batch (do not change today’s spec).
- Herb browning (Champagne-herb): Keep herbs dry and cold; fold in at service day. If browning, discard.
- Allergen cross-contact: Use clean tools and cambros. If contaminated, discard and remake.

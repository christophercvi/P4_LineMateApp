# Roasted Chicken Stock

Owner: Samuel Okafor (Sous Chef, Prep)
Station: Prep
Batch: 40 qt
Summary: 40 qt batch, 6 h simmer, cool with ice wands per the cooling SOP.

## Purpose, Yield, and Standards
- Build a clean, roasted chicken base for sauces, soups, and risotto.
- No salt added in production.
- Expected yield: 32 qt. Report yields under 28 qt on a ticket.
- Color: deep gold/amber. Aroma: roasted chicken and vegetables, no bitterness.
- Texture: light body, no grease slick, no cloudiness.

## Ingredients and Ratios
- Chicken backs: 30 lb, roasted.
- Cold water: 44 qt to start.
- Mirepoix: 6 lb total (onion-heavy; large rough cut).
- Sachet: house aromatic sachet, tied tight in cheesecloth. Add at hour 4 only.

Tip: Do not add tomato paste to this stock. Roasting provides the color.

## Equipment and Setup
- Convection oven set to 425°F.
- Full-size sheet pans with racks (for roasting), high-heat spatula, ladle, skimmer.
- Large stockpot suitable for 44 qt water plus bones (leave headspace).
- Heat source capable of holding a bare simmer.
- China cap and fine-mesh strainer.
- 4-in hotel pans or 2-gal buckets for chilling and storage (never the full stockpot).
- Ice wands (frozen solid), ice bath bin, thermometer with sanitizing wipes.
- Labels and tape for batch ID, volume, and initials.

## Pre-Flight Checklist
- Bones thawed, dry, and ready (30 lb).
- Oven at 425°F; racks middle-high.
- Ice wands frozen; ice bath set.
- All containers, strainers, and tools clean and ready.
- Space cleared in walk-in for shallow pans/buckets.

## Procedure
1. Roast the bones
   - Spread 30 lb chicken backs on racks over sheet pans. Do not crowd.
   - Roast at 425°F until deep golden (≈ 45 min). Rotate pans midway if needed.
   - Drain rendered fat; reserve separately for discard once cooled.
2. Start the stock
   - Transfer roasted backs to the stockpot. Cover with 44 qt cold water.
   - Bring to a bare simmer. Skim steadily as foam rises. Do not boil.
3. Build flavor and hold a gentle simmer
   - Maintain a bare simmer with minimal movement. Skim as needed.
   - At hour 4, add mirepoix (6 lb) and the sachet. Push under the surface.
   - Simmer 6 h total. Keep the liquid clear; adjust heat to avoid boiling.
4. Strain
   - Kill the heat. Lift out bulk solids with a spider, then strain through a china cap into a clean vessel.
   - Final pass through a fine-mesh strainer for clarity.
   - Strain into 4-in hotel pans or 2-gal buckets, never the full stockpot.
5. Cool and log
   - Cool immediately using ice wands — follow SOP: Cooling Hot Foods and log temps.
   - Stir with sanitized wand, swap in fresh as they melt. Use an ice bath around pans if needed.
6. Store
   - Once ≤ 41°F, cover, label with product name, volume, initials, and time in.
   - Hold refrigerated. Use FIFO.

## Cooling Targets and Actions
Follow SOP: Cooling Hot Foods. Use this table to verify and take action.

| Checkpoint | Target temp | Max time window | What to do if above target |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Start (post-strain) | 135°F+ | — | Insert ice wands immediately, set in ice bath, vented |
| Stage 1 | 70°F | 2 hours | Add fresh ice wands, stir, split into more shallow pans, increase ice bath |
| Stage 2 | 41°F | 4 more hours (6 hours total) | Replace wands/ice, move to colder shelf, recheck every 30 min |

- Take and log temps in the cooling log at Start, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h, and when ≤ 41°F.

## Quality Control, Yield, and Tickets
- Skimming: If scum builds, you’re too hot or not skimming enough. Reduce heat.
- Clarity: If cloudy, check that it never boiled and that strainers were clean.
- Flavor: Balanced roasted chicken, light veg. If bitter, vegetables roasted or simmered too long.
- Yield: Expected 32 qt. Report yields under 28 qt on a ticket.
  - Ticket must include: batch name, your initials, start time, simmer time, observed yield, evaporation cause (excess boil, uncovered too high heat), and any spill note.

## Troubleshooting and What-If
- It started boiling: Drop heat immediately. Add 1–2 qt cold water to settle. Resume bare simmer and skim.
- Didn’t reach 70°F in 2 h: Split into more shallow pans, add fresh ice wands, increase ice bath. Notify Samuel Okafor.
- Didn’t reach 41°F in 6 h total: Do not serve. Notify Samuel Okafor for discard decision per SOP.
- Yield low but flavor strong: Top with hot water only if within simmer window and approved by Samuel Okafor; otherwise, keep as is and adjust in recipes.
- Oil slick on top: Let chill; lift solid fat cap before use.
- No ice wands available: Stop and make ice wands or set up an aggressive ice bath; do not park hot stock in the walk-in.

## Cleanup and Waste
- Cool and discard fat into the designated waste bin, never the drain.
- Veg trim and spent bones go to compost or solid waste per house bins.
- Wash, rinse, sanitize all pans, strainers, and tools. Dry and store.
- Clean oven sheet pans while still warm; deglaze stuck bits into the stock during simmer if they are not burnt.

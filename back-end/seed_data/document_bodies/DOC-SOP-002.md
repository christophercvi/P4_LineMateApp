# Allergen Handling & Station Separation

## 1) Overview & Owner
- Document owner: Samuel Okafor (Sous Chef, Prep)
- Summary: Purple boards and tools, dedicated fryer for gluten-free, ticket flag “ALLERGY”.
- Goal: Zero cross-contact. Clear communication, clean tools, strict separation on Prep and across the line.

## 2) The big nine
Milk, egg, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame.

## 3) Rules on the line
1. Any ticket flagged **ALLERGY** is called out loud by expo and acknowledged by the station.
2. Use **purple** cutting boards, knives and tongs. Purple tools never leave the allergen caddy.
3. Wash hands and change gloves before starting an allergy order.
4. **Fryer #3 is gluten-free only.** Nothing breaded goes into it, ever.
5. Plate on a clean tray; expo delivers allergy orders separately with a purple pick.

## 4) Prep station setup and separation
- Allergen caddy (purple): 2 chef knives, 1 paring knife, 12" tongs, spatula, peeler, portion scoops, thermometer sleeve, sanitizer test strips. Store sealed, top shelf of prep rack, left of hand sink.
- Purple boards stacked vertical in a rack; never mixed into general board stack.
- Single-use items: deli sheets, 1/6-pan liners, pastry bags, and labeled squeeze bottles dedicated to allergy work.
- Designated space: top-left third pan in the lowboy is reserved for allergen-safe mise; keep lidded.
- Labels: use white day-dot with “ALLERGY” in block letters and the specific allergen(s) to avoid (e.g., “ALLERGY—NO WHEAT”).
- Hand sink: keep stocked with soap and paper towels; no storage in basin; hands washed before handling caddy.
- Wipe buckets: one QUAT bucket for general, one separate QUAT bucket marked “PURPLE—ALLERGY ONLY”.

## 5) Allergy ticket flow (Prep)
Follow this every time an allergy ticket is called.

1. Expo calls the ticket flagged ALLERGY. Acknowledge out loud.
2. Clear and sanitize a 24"x24" space. Remove all open mise and uncovered pans.
3. Wash hands (20 seconds), dry, and change gloves.
4. Pull purple board and purple tools from the allergen caddy. Tools never leave this zone.
5. Verify ingredients. Read the recipe card and supplier spec sheet for the items you plan to use.
6. Set fresh containers: new deli cups, new liners, clean lids.
7. Prepare only the allergy components. Keep raw and RTE items separated. Use deli sheets for transfers.
8. If hot items are required from another station, communicate “ALLERGY—NEED CLEAN PAN/UTENSIL” to that station. Wait for confirmation.
9. Assemble on a clean tray. Do not stack with other trays. Place finished item for expo with purple pick.
10. Replace purple tools in caddy. Sanitize the zone. Wash hands and change gloves again before resuming regular prep.

If you hit any uncertainty at any step, stop and ask the Sous Chef. Never guess an ingredient — check the recipe card or the supplier spec sheet.

## 6) Cleaning and sanitizing protocol
- Sink setup: wash at 110°F minimum with detergent, rinse, then sanitize.
- Sanitizer (QUAT): 200–400 ppm, 30-second contact time. Verify with test strips before use and every 2 hours.
- Chlorine option (if QUAT unavailable): 50–100 ppm, 7-second contact time. Use test strips.
- Air-dry tools and boards; never towel-dry allergen tools.
- Between tasks: sanitize work surface, knife, handle, and board. Change gloves after sanitizing.
- Dish machine: use the high-temp cycle; final rinse gauge must hit 160°F or above at the manifold; use heat-sensitive label to confirm.
- Spills of allergen-containing ingredients: stop, block the area, use clean paper towels, bag and trash, then sanitize twice.

## 7) Dedicated Fryer #3 (gluten-free only)
- Use only for gluten-free items. Nothing breaded goes into it, ever.
- Skim before service and between drops. Use a skimmer reserved for Fryer #3.
- Filter oil per schedule; never connect filtration to non-GF fryers at the same time.
- Verify with gluten test strips if there is any doubt post-filtration. If positive, drain, clean, and refill.

Table — Fryer #3 quick rules:
- Assignment: Gluten-free only
- Tools: Dedicated skimmer and spider, labeled purple tape
- Filter: At end of each service
- Oil change: As needed if dark, smoky, or test indicates contamination
- Holding: Use clean, lined hotel pan and purple-taped tongs

## 8) Storage, receiving, and mise for allergen-safe items
- Receiving: Keep sealed cases of gluten-free or nut-free items intact; mark outer case “ALLERGY—DO NOT OPEN OUTSIDE PREP”.
- Dry storage: Top shelves, away from bulk flour, nuts, seeds, and breading.
- Walk-in: Store covered and above raw proteins and above allergen-heavy items. No stacking under open flour or crumb bins.
- Mise building: Do first thing after sanitizing the zone; use new liners and scoops; label “ALLERGY” with details.
- Batch prep: Small batches only; discard if cross-contact is suspected.
- Squeeze bottles: Freshly washed and sanitized; no topping off.

## 9) What to do if / troubleshooting
- If cross-contact happens (dropped on wrong board, touched non-purple tool, splash from breaded fryer):
  1. Stop. Discard the item and any exposed mise.
  2. Wash hands, change gloves, sanitize zone and tools.
  3. Inform Expo and the Sous Chef immediately.
  4. Restart the order from the top with clean tools and fresh ingredients.
- If a guest adds an allergy mid-service: treat as a new allergy ticket. Re-make; do not modify the old plate.
- If purple tools are missing: stop and ask the Sous Chef. Do not substitute standard tools.
- If sanitizer is out of range: remake to correct ppm before proceeding.
- If in doubt
  - Stop and ask the Sous Chef. Never guess an ingredient — check the recipe card or the supplier spec sheet.

# Incident: Fryer Oil Delivery Short — Grill Station

## 1) Summary (As Received)
- Tuesday delivery from Harbor Foodservice arrived with 2 of 6 cases of canola frying oil. Invoice was signed without noting the shortage.
- Current stock: 1 case after Wednesday's change. Par is 4 cases.
- Linked ticket: TKT-005.

## 2) Photo Description (What the picture shows)
- Pallet at receiving with exactly 2 sealed cases of canola frying oil on the floor next to the walk-in door.
- Open invoice on a clipboard with a signature but no “short” notation or line-through.
- Grill station fryers visible in background; one filter cart staged nearby.
- Time stamp overlay from the prep area camera; ambient floor is dry and clear. No spill, no damage noted on cases.

## 3) Impact on Grill Station Ops
- Stock below par (1 case on-hand vs par 4) risks:
  - Inability to perform scheduled oil changes and top-offs.
  - Faster oil degradation if changes are deferred.
  - Potential quality drift (color, off-flavors) and increased acrylamide formation if oil past discard point.
- Immediate menu risk: fried sides and proteins may be restricted if oil cannot be maintained.

## 4) Immediate Containment Steps (Do Now)
1. Secure remaining 1 case in dry storage; log lot and case count in TKT-005.
2. Inspect all fryers; filter and skim to extend life. Target fry temps per station spec: 350°F unless item spec says otherwise.
3. Prioritize cleanest vat for high-visibility items; consolidate volume across vats if needed.
4. Update line-up board: “Oil tight — prioritize low-crumb items; shake-off breading.”
5. Notify MOD and Priya Nair (Sous Chef, Grill). Reference TKT-005.
6. Call Harbor Foodservice immediately to report shortage; request rush fill or same-week add-on.
7. Document call name/time and RMA/confirmation in TKT-005.

## 5) Receiving Correction Procedure (Prevent Repeat)
1. Count cases on pallet before breaking wrap; match to invoice line.
2. If short, write “SHORT — received 2 of 6” on invoice, circle item, line through missing qty.
3. Get driver initials next to the note; take photo of pallet + annotated invoice.
4. Refuse backorder unless confirmed delivery window is acceptable; note on invoice.
5. Update inventory sheet and TKT-005 with actual received qty and photos.
6. File annotated invoice in vendor binder; copy to MOD.

## 6) Oil Management Until Replenishment
- Filter schedule: after each service and when crumbs visible; cool oil to safe handling temp before filtering.
- Discard rule of thumb: when oil smokes at set temp, darkens, or fails fry test (slow bubble, greasy finish).
- Reduce crumb load:
  - Shake-off breading over drip pans.
  - Fry in baskets, not free-drop.
  - Salt and sauce off the fryer.
- Menu triage:
  - Prioritize low-fat-uptake items.
  - Batch fry only if it maintains quality; avoid overload.

## 7) Par, Reorder, and Usage Guide
| Item                          | Par (cases) | On Hand (cases) | Variance | Reorder Trigger | Notes                          |
|-------------------------------|-------------|------------------|----------|-----------------|--------------------------------|
| Canola frying oil (cases)     | 4           | 1                | -3       | ≤ 2 cases       | Place rush order if at 1 case. |

- Typical usage: 1–2 cases per heavy service day with full fryer changes; 0.5–1 case with filter-only days.
- Reorder when at 2 cases to buffer delivery slip-ups.

## 8) Communication and Documentation
- Service log: record each filter/change, amount added (qt), observed oil condition.
- Ticket updates: append photos, annotated invoice, vendor call details to TKT-005.
- Pre-service huddle: brief team on conservation steps and any 86/limited items.

## 9) If Shortage Persists
- Check with Prep and Pastry for unopened compatible canola oil; do not mix with animal fats or flavored oils.
- If subbing fry medium, clear with Priya Nair; verify smoke point ≥ 400°F and neutral flavor.
- If quality cannot be held, 86 affected fried items and notify FOH with precise language and ETA from vendor.

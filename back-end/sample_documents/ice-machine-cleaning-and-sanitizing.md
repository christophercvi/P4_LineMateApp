# Ice Machine Cleaning and Sanitizing

## 1) Scope & Owner
- Station: Front of House
- Equipment: FOH ice machine, bin, scoop, scoop holder, drain line
- Owner: Grace Liu (FOH Lead)
- Goal: Safe, clean ice with no off-odors, slime, or scale. Follow this SOP for daily use, checks each shift, and monthly descale/sanitize.

## 2) Safety & Lockout Before Any Cleaning
1. Stop service ice. Pull backup ice from BOH if needed.
2. Turn machine OFF at its power switch.
3. Unplug the unit. If hardwired or you can’t reach the plug, ask MOD to shut off the breaker.
4. Close the water supply valve to the unit.
5. Hang a “Do Not Use—Cleaning” tag on the machine and bin.
6. Wear cut-resistant gloves for panels, and dish gloves and eye protection for chemicals.

## 3) Daily Use Rules (Ice Handling)
- Hands and food never go in the bin. Ice is a ready-to-eat food.
- Use the designated scoop only. No cups, pitchers, or glassware.
- Store scoop in the covered scoop holder, handle up, not buried in ice.
- Keep bin lid closed when not scooping.
- If a glass breaks near the bin, stop using the ice. Close bin, call MOD. Discard all ice in bin and sanitize per Section 6 (sanitize-only steps).
- Wipe external touch points (bin handle, scoop handle, buttons) every two hours with sanitizer.

## 4) Leak & Drain Checks (Each Opening and Mid-Shift)
1. Check floor around machine and under bin for drip or puddles.
2. Inspect drain line: no kinks, no standing water in the line, air gap clear.
3. Look inside bin: no pooled water, slime, or unusual frost clumps.
4. Listen for abnormal cycling or hammering in the water feed.
5. If you find a leak or backup, follow Section 5.

## 5) If the Machine Leaks, Backs Up, or Ice Smells/Tastes Off
- Immediate actions:
  1. Turn OFF the machine and unplug.
  2. Close water valve.
  3. Post “Do Not Use—Out of Service” tag.
  4. For leaks: place a bus tub to catch drips; keep floor dry with wet floor sign.
  5. For off-odor/taste: discard all ice in bin.
- Notify: Grace Liu (FOH Lead) and MOD. Submit a maintenance ticket with photos (leak source, drain, bin interior).
- Do not restart until inspected, cleaned, and cleared by Grace or MOD. If drain is blocked or leak source unknown, call approved service.

## 6) Monthly Descale & Sanitize (or If Off-Odor/Slime Appears)
Perform with lockout (Section 2). Use nickel‑safe ice machine descaler and food‑contact no‑rinse sanitizer.
1. Remove ice: Scoop out and discard all ice from bin.
2. Disassemble food-contact parts per machine design: water curtain/distributor, spray bar, cube guides, bin baffle, scoop holder. Keep screws in a ramekin.
3. Pre-clean: Wash parts and bin interior with hot water and dish soap. Use a nylon brush. Rinse with clean water.
4. Descale internal water circuit (follow label):
   - Restore power and water briefly only if required to circulate cleaner per manufacturer, otherwise soak removed parts in descaler.
   - Mix nickel-safe descaler per label (common: 8 oz per 1 gal warm water). Never exceed label.
   - Soak removable parts 15–30 minutes until scale loosens. Brush and rinse well.
   - For fixed components (evaporator, distribution trough): with power OFF/unplugged, apply descaler using squeeze bottle/brush; avoid wiring. Dwell per label, then rinse thoroughly with potable water.
5. Sanitize all food-contact surfaces:
   - Mix sanitizer per table below. Flood/spray interior bin walls, baffle, drain, and all parts. Ensure full wetting.
   - Keep surfaces visibly wet for full contact time. Air-dry; do not towel dry.
6. Bin drain flush: Pour 1 qt sanitizer down bin drain to contact the line and air gap.
7. Exterior wipe-down: Clean and sanitize door, buttons, handles, and scoop holder.
8. Reassemble parts securely.
9. Restart and purge first harvest:
   - Open water valve, plug in, turn ON.
   - Discard first two harvests of ice to remove residues.
10. Taste and smell check: Ice should be neutral. If off-odor remains, repeat sanitize and call service.

## 7) Sanitizer Guide (Use One Option)
| Sanitizer | Mix Ratio | Target PPM | Contact Time | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Quaternary ammonium | As labeled (typ. 1 tablet/1 gal or 1 oz/2 gal) | 200–400 ppm | 1 minute | Use test strips to confirm ppm; no rinse required |
| Chlorine (unscented) | 1/2 oz per 1 gal cool water | 50–100 ppm | 1 minute | Use test strips; air-dry; avoid corrosion on stainless overuse |

Follow the product label if it differs; never exceed ppm. Always use potable water.

## 8) Task Schedule & Who Does What
| Task | Who | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Scoop storage and touch-point sanitizing | FOH on duty | Every 2 hours and as needed |
| Leak and drain check | Opening FOH + Mid-shift FOH | Twice per service |
| Bin interior sanitize (no descale) | FOH Lead or MOD | Weekly |
| Full descale and sanitize | Grace Liu or trained FOH | Monthly or if off-odor/slime |
| Service call if leak/backup persists | MOD | As needed |

## 9) Records & Verification
- Log each cleaning in the FOH Equipment Log: task, initials, time, sanitizer ppm, and any issues.
- Keep test strips for quat and chlorine at the host stand caddy; replace when expired or if color pads fade.
- MOD verifies monthly descale completion and first-harvest discard.

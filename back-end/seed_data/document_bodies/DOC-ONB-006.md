# Opening & Closing Checklists

## Scope & Owner
- Station: Front of House (FOH) service, expo, beverage, dish drop, guest areas
- Document owner: Elena Petrova (Kitchen Manager)
- Purpose: Per-station opening and closing, including temperature and chemical logs; FOH verifies cross-station items that affect guests

## Chemical & Temp Reference
Short notes: Use fresh strips daily. Replace buckets every 4 hours or when dirty.

| Item/Area              | Target/Range             | How to Check                         | Action If Out of Range                |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk-in / reach-in     | ≤41°F                    | Ambient and product probe            | Call KM; move product to coldest unit; tag unit |
| Cold hold on pass      | ≤41°F                    | Probe top item each pan              | Ice bath/swap pans; escalate if unit warm |
| Hot hold (soup/sauce)  | ≥135°F                   | Probe center after stir              | Reheat to 165°F then hold ≥135°F      |
| Sanitizer buckets (quat) | 200–400 ppm             | Quat test strip                      | Remix to spec; replace towels          |
| Dish machine chlorine  | 50–100 ppm at ≥120°F     | Chlorine strip on final rack         | Pause service ware; refill/prime; retest |

## Procedures
1) Temp logging (walk-in/reach-in)
   1. Wash hands; use calibrated probe.
   2. Read ambient, then probe one ready-to-eat item per unit.
   3. Log immediately; report any unit above 41°F.

2) Mix and test sanitizer buckets
   1. Fill with warm water, add quat to reach 200–400 ppm.
   2. Label area (bar, dining, expo, dish).
   3. Test with strip; change every 4 hours or sooner if cloudy.

3) Dish machine chlorine test
   1. Run machine; send a plain plate through twice.
   2. Test final rinse with chlorine strip; target 50–100 ppm at ≥120°F.
   3. If low, pause warewashing, prime sanitizer, retest, then resume.

4) Fryer TPM tested and logged
   1. Ask Grill lead to meter each vat at start of day.
   2. Record “TPM tested and logged” once Grill confirms green/OK.

5) Closing floor and drains
   1. Dry scrape; sweep; degrease; mop; squeegee to drains.
   2. Pull mats; empty drain baskets; leave floors air-dry.

## What To Do If
- Walk-in/reach-in above 41°F: Do not serve RTE from that unit; move product; call Elena Petrova; log corrective action.
- Sanitizer out of range: Remix; retrain anyone using dirty towels; recheck in 5 minutes.
- Dish machine fails chlorine test: Stop sending glass/plate; switch to 3-comp sink set to wash, rinse, sanitize (quat 200–400 ppm) until fixed.
- Fryer TPM out of range or unknown: Do not serve fried items until Grill confirms oil OK; notify FOH lead and Elena.
- Gas not off at grill: Notify Grill immediately; if unattended, call Elena; do not leave until confirmed off.

# Grill Deep Clean & Degreasing

Document owner: Priya Nair (Sous Chef, Grill)

Summary: Weekly degrease of grill, flat-top and hood filters; monthly hood service.

## Scope, Frequency, and Standards
- Applies to Grill station equipment: charbroiler grates, flat-top, drip pans, under-grill, and hood filters.
- Weekly: full degrease of grill and flat-top; hood filters run through dish machine; grease traps emptied and cleaned.
- Monthly: hood service clean (duct lips, plenum faces, fan shroud exterior).
- The hood suppression system is inspected by the vendor every 6 months (sticker on the hood).

## Safety, Lockout, and PPE
- Wear cut-resistant gloves, heat-resistant gloves, apron, eye protection.
- Gas off = at the line valve. Confirm pilots out and knobs in OFF.
- Never apply water or degreaser above 150°F. Steam and caustic burn risk.
- Keep electrical cords and outlets away from wet zones. Floor is slippery—place wet floor signs.

## Tools, Chemicals, and Setup
- Tools: grill tongs, wire brush, grill bricks, scraper, scouring pads, nylon brush, 5-gal bus tubs, towels, squeegee, razor for carbon edges, buckets, measuring cup.
- Chemicals: heavy-duty food-service degreaser (label-use dilution), hot rinse water, neutralizing rinse (clean water), high-smoke-point oil (canola).
- Set up: two-bay system for grates (degrease soak, rinse), dish racks for filters, lined waste bin for grease solids.

## Weekly Deep Clean Procedure
1. Shut off gas at the line valve; let surfaces cool below 150°F.
   - Verify with surface thermometer or instant-read at plate edge.
   - Pull all food, pans, and utensils off the station.

2. Remove grates, soak in degreaser 30 min, scrub, rinse, oil lightly.
   - Fully submerge grates in bus tub with degreaser. Agitate halfway through.
   - Scrub with wire brush, then nylon to finish. Rinse with hot water. Dry completely.
   - Wipe a thin film of oil to prevent rust; no pooling.

3. Scrape flat-top, apply grill brick, season with oil.
   - Push debris to grease trough. Empty trough before it overflows.
   - Use grill brick with light oil to lift carbon. Wipe to a clean, light sheen.
   - Final thin oil coat; no smoke point testing until next heat-up.

4. Hood filters go through the dish machine on the pots cycle.
   - Knock off loose grease into waste bin before loading.
   - Load filters vertically, staggered for spray reach. Run pots cycle.
   - Air-dry fully; do not reinstall dripping filters.

5. Empty and clean grease traps; log on the cleaning chart.
   - Wear PPE. Lift screens; remove solids to lined bin. Wash, rinse, and air-dry parts.
   - Wipe trap cavity; no standing water left. Reassemble correctly.
   - Sign and time-stamp the cleaning chart.

## Monthly Hood Service (Cleaning Tasks)
- With line off and cool, remove filters and wipe the plenum faces and duct lips reachable without tools using degreaser and towels; do not spray into ducts.
- Clean the hood light lenses and trim; dry fully before reinstalling.
- Wipe the fan shroud exterior and accessible seams; do not access the roof or interior fans.
- Inspect filter frames for warping and broken mesh; tag any failures for replacement.
- Reinstall filters in correct order, baffles vertical and arrow up.

## Quality Targets and Key Limits
| Item | Target/Limit | How to Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Surface temp before chemicals | Below 150°F | Surface or IR thermometer |
| Grate soak time | 30 min | Timer set at start |
| Filter wash | Pots cycle | Machine display/cycle selection |
| Oil film | Thin, even, no pooling | Visual check with towel |

## Reassembly and Heat-Up
1. Reinstall dry grates and dry hood filters.
2. Open gas at the line valve. Relight pilots per station procedure.
3. Bring surfaces to medium heat to set oil seasoning; no food for 10 minutes.
4. Wipe any residual oil drips from troughs and edges.

## What to Do If
- Filter won’t come clean after pots cycle:
  - Run one additional pots cycle. If still greasy, soak in degreaser 15 minutes, scrub, rinse, then rerun. Tag for replacement if mesh is clogged or bent.
- Degreaser contact causes white haze on flat-top:
  - Rinse, dry, re-brick with light oil, re-season. Avoid over-concentrated solution next time.
- Gas odor detected:
  - Do not relight. Close line valve, evacuate immediate area, notify MOD and Priya Nair.
- Grease trap smell persists after cleaning:
  - Recheck assembly orientation, clean surrounding floor drain grates, and pour 1 qt hot water down the trap to flush.

## End-of-Job Checks and Documentation
- Station passes white-towel test: swipe towel comes off light, not brown/black.
- No drips in hood plenum; filters seated flat; lights clean.
- Floors around grill degreased and rinsed; wet floor signs removed when dry.
- Complete cleaning chart entries for grates, flat-top, filters, grease traps. Initials required.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Spraying degreaser onto hot metal above 150°F.
- Reinstalling wet filters or grates (rust, drip hazards).
- Pooling oil on the flat-top (flares, off flavors).
- Skipping grease trap cleaning; it backs up under heat.

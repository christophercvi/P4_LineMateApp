# Laminated Croissant Dough

## Scope & Ownership
- Station: Pastry. Document owner: Aiko Tanaka (Pastry Lead).
- Product: classic laminated croissant dough; three single folds; proof at 78°F / 80% RH for 2 h.
- Note: Proofing settings listed are for the old Doyon proofer; verify on the new unit.
- Target: clean layers, honeycomb crumb, even browning at 375°F convection, 18 min.
- Allergen alert: dairy, gluten. Label racks and sheet pans.

## Ingredients & Scaling (Détrempe)
- Détrempe formula (keep exact weights):
  - Bread flour 1000 g (35.3 oz) · water 280 g (9.9 oz) · milk 280 g (9.9 oz)
  - sugar 110 g (3.9 oz) · salt 20 g (0.7 oz) · osmotolerant yeast 12 g (0.4 oz) · butter 50 g (1.8 oz)
- Lamination fat: Butter block 560 g (19.8 oz), pliable at 55°F.
- Batch yields ~20–24 standard croissants from 9 × 24 cm triangles (size-dependent).
- Use osmotolerant yeast; do not sub instant/active. Keep butter unsalted, 82% fat or higher.

## Equipment & Setup
- Mixer with dough hook; sheeter; rolling pin backup; thermometer; hygrometer; timer.
- Cold table or sheet pan for rests at 38°F; plastic wrap; bench brush; sharp wheel or knife.
- Proofer: settings reference the old Doyon proofer (78°F, 80% RH). New proofer: match those conditions using independent thermometer/hygrometer.
- Ovens: convection set to 375°F; verify with oven thermometer.
- Butter block prep: pound between parchment to even thickness; check 55°F pliability (bends without cracking, not greasy).

## Mixing Détrempe — Procedure
1. Chill milk and water to 38–40°F. Cube butter (50 g) cold.
2. In mixer, combine flour, sugar, salt, osmotolerant yeast. Add liquids; mix low until shaggy.
3. Add 50 g butter; mix low to medium-low until smooth, gluten just formed, 4–6 min. Dough should be cool and not sticky.
4. Shape into a 10 × 14 in slab, 1 in thick. Wrap tight. Refrigerate to 38°F, minimum 30 min, up to 2 h.
5. Keep dough and butter within 5–7°F of each other for lock-in (target dough ~40–45°F, butter 55°F).

## Lamination — Procedure
1. Butter block 560 g, pliable at 55°F. Sheet détrempe to fit block with 1/2 in margins.
2. Lock in, then three single folds, 30 min rest at 38°F between folds. Wrap between rests.
3. Keep bench lightly floured; brush off excess flour before each fold. Maintain rectangle corners sharp.
4. If butter smears, dough too warm—chill 10–15 min. If butter cracks, warm 2–3 min at room temp.
5. After final rest, sheet to 4 mm; keep dough below 60°F throughout.

## Sheeting, Cutting, Shaping
- Sheet to 4 mm; cut 9 × 24 cm triangles. Use sharp wheel; no dragging.
- Notch base, stretch gently, roll snug without crushing layers; point underneath.
- Place on lined sheet, tip tucked. Pan with 2 in spacing for proof/oven spring.
- Egg wash only after proof (avoid sealing layers).

## Proofing & Baking
- Proof at 78°F and 80% RH for 2 h (settings for the old Doyon proofer).
- New proofer: adjust controls to achieve same temp/RH by thermometer/hygrometer; do not exceed 80°F.
- Bake: Egg wash once. Bake 375°F convection, 18 min. Rotate at 10 min if needed.
- Doneness: deep golden, no pale shoulders, internal temp 205–210°F.

## Process Controls & Reference
- Temperatures and holds:
  | Step                  | Target             | Hold/Time              |
  |-----------------------|--------------------|------------------------|
  | Butter block          | 55°F pliable       | Use immediately        |
  | Rest between folds    | 38°F               | 30 min each rest       |
  | Final proof (old unit)| 78°F / 80% RH      | 2 h                    |
  | Bake                  | 375°F convection   | 18 min                 |
- Cold chain: keep dough ≤41°F outside of proofing; minimize bench time.

## Quality Checks & Troubleshooting
- Underproof: tight crumb, burst seams. Add 15–20 min proof; verify 80% RH.
- Overproof: collapse, butter leak. Reduce proof by 10–15 min; check proofer temp not over 78°F.
- Butter leakage in bake: layers compromised. Review butter temp at lock-in and rest discipline.
- Uneven color: thin egg wash; ensure even oven load; rotate once.
- If new proofer can’t hit 80% RH: place pans of hot water; avoid direct steam blasting. Adjust time to visual cues (jiggle, visible layers, slight swell).

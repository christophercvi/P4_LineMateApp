# Incident: Blast Chiller Temperature Excursion

## 1) Document Owner and Scope
- Owner: Samuel Okafor (Sous Chef, Prep)
- Station: Prep
- Applies to: Any cooked, TCS prep item entering the blast chiller; this incident focuses on a chili batch.

## 2) Incident Summary (as recorded)
- The 6 gal chili batch read 88°F at the 2 h mark (limit 70°F). It was reheated to 165°F and re-cooled in 2-in pans with ice wands, passing both checks.
- Suspected cause: blast chiller not pulling down; display showed error E4. See ticket TKT-017.

## 3) Food Safety Limits (cooling and reheat)
- Cooling must meet both limits below. If either fails, reheat to 165°F and restart cooling.
- Reheat of previously cooled food: to 165°F within 2 h, then recool per limits.

| Step | Limit | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 135°F to 70°F | Within 2 h | ≤ 70°F at 2 h | First cooling check |
| 70°F to 41°F | Within 4 h more (total ≤ 6 h) | ≤ 41°F by 6 h | Second cooling check |
| Reheat (if cooling failed) | 165°F | Within 2 h | Stir, verify center temp |

## 4) Immediate Containment (what was done)
1. Stopped the chill at the 2 h check when the batch probed 88°F (limit 70°F).
2. Returned chili to stove; reheated rapidly to 165°F, stirring the batch to heat evenly.
3. Divided into shallow 2-in hotel pans, inserted ice wands, and reloaded to blast chiller.
4. Verified both cooling checks passed on the second attempt.
5. Logged readings and opened equipment ticket TKT-017 for error E4.

## 5) Re-cooling Procedure for Chili (standardize this)
1. After reheat to 165°F, move product to clean, sanitized 2-in pans.
2. Fill pans no deeper than 2 in; leave headspace for airflow.
3. Insert sanitized ice wand in each pan; agitate every 10–15 min to break heat pockets.
4. Place pans in blast chiller with at least 1 in clearance on all sides; do not stack.
5. Set chiller to hard chill program; verify fan and door seal.
6. Probe the coldest-recovering pan:
   - Record at 2 h (target ≤ 70°F).
   - Record at 6 h total cooling (target ≤ 41°F).
7. Remove ice wands once product hits ≤ 70°F to avoid over-dilution; replace lids and continue to 41°F.
8. Label and move to walk-in. Store above raw and below ready-to-eat garnishes.

## 6) Monitoring and Logging (how to check and record)
- Use a calibrated digital probe; sanitize between pans.
- Check multiple pans: probe center and thickest area, avoiding wand contact.
- Record actual times and temperatures; note corrective actions if limits missed.

| Checkpoint | Time From Start | Target Temp | Record Actual | Initials |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start chill (post-portion) | 0 h | 135°F or lower entering unit | | |
| First check | 2 h | ≤ 70°F | | |
| Second check | ≤ 6 h total | ≤ 41°F | | |

- If any pan fails, treat the whole batch as failed for corrective action.

## 7) Troubleshooting: Blast Chiller Not Pulling Down
If product temp stalls or rises, or display shows error:
- Error observed: E4 on display. Ticket reference: TKT-017.
- Quick checks (do in order):
  1. Verify door fully closed; gasket intact; no gaps.
  2. Reduce load: no stacking; 1 in clearance around pans.
  3. Switch to hard chill mode; confirm fans blowing.
  4. Check that pans are ≤ 2 in deep; redistribute if deeper.
  5. Confirm ice wands are fully frozen and inserted; replace warm wands.
  6. Probe ambient in chiller; if ambient > 38–40°F after 10 min with door closed, escalate.
- If E4 persists or ambient not dropping:
  - Stop using the unit for active cooling.
  - Move product to ice bath plus shallow pans and ice wands to meet limits.
  - Notify Sous Chef and log details; attach to TKT-017.

## 8) What To Do If Limits Are Missed (decision tree)
- At 2 h > 70°F (as in this incident: 88°F):
  1. Reheat to 165°F within 2 h.
  2. Re-cool in 2-in pans with ice wands and blast chiller or ice bath.
  3. Recheck at 2 h and 6 h. If both pass, keep.
- At 6 h > 41°F:
  - Discard the batch. Do not attempt to salvage.
- If reheat cannot reach 165°F promptly or quality prevents safe reheat:
  - Discard.

## 9) Prevention and Station Controls
- Batch size: Limit hot-fill batches for dense items (like chili) to pan depths of 2 in.
- Airflow: Never cover hot pans; vent lids until ≤ 70°F.
- Load discipline: Stagger loads to avoid overloading the blast chiller.
- Stir/agitate: Use ice wands and stir every 10–15 min until ≤ 70°F.
- Probe habit: Always set a 2 h timer at start of cooling; set a second timer for 6 h.
- Calibration: Verify thermometers at ice point each shift start.
- Maintenance: Report any alarms, long pull-down times, icing on coils, or door seal issues immediately with a ticket.
